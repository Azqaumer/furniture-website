# Store Owner Admin Guide — FURNI

Welcome to your store dashboard! Below are step-by-step instructions on how to manage your products, collections, customer orders, and messages.

---

## 1. Accessing the Admin Dashboard
1. Open your browser and go to: `https://your-domain.com/admin.html`
2. In the **Admin Key** field, enter your secure password.
3. Click **Load Data**.

---

## 2. Managing Collections
* **Add a Collection:** Scroll to **Collections Manager**, type a collection name (e.g., "Coastal Living"), and click **Add Collection**.
* **Edit Hero Image:** Paste an image URL into the **Hero Image URL** field next to any collection and click **Save**.

---

## 3. Managing Products
* **Add a Product:** Click **+ Add New Product**, fill in the details (Name, Price, Stock, Category, Collection, Image URL, Description), and click **Save Changes**.
* **Edit / Update Stock:** Click **Edit** next to any product in the list, update your price or stock quantity, and click **Save Changes**.

---

## 4. Viewing Orders & Messages
* **Orders:** The **Orders** section displays all incoming customer purchases, including order totals and customer contact info.
* **Messages:** Customer inquiries sent through the contact form appear under the **Messages** section.
