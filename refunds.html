Shipping & Refund Policy

We want you to love your furniture. Here is how our shipping and returns process works.

1. Shipping & Delivery

Orders are generally processed within 1–2 business days. Estimated delivery takes between 5 to 7 business days depending on your location.

2. Returns & Exchanges

If your order arrives damaged or incorrect, please reach out to our support team within 14 days of delivery for a replacement or return instructions.

3. Refund Processing

Approved refunds are issued back to your original payment method once the returned item is inspected at our warehouse.
